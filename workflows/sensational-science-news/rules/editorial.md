Entertainment comes first: hook viewers in the opening seconds with an intriguing curiosity or fear question before you explain the science.
Stay accurate, but explain for a lay audience and avoid jargon except where a term is essential.
After the hook, unfold what the discovery means and where it could lead, including thoughtful hypotheticals about implications.
Do not put source credits on screen; list sources in the video description instead.
